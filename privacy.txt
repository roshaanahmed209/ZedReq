1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?
Information You Provide Directly
In Short: We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering, using our Services, or contacting us.


The personal information we collect may include:
Full name and username
Email address and phone number
Job titles and professional history
Educational background, skills, and certifications
Career goals and preferences you share on your profile
Account passwords and authentication credentials
Payment instrument details (processed and stored by Paddle — see their Privacy Policy at https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy)

Information From Social Logins
If you choose to register or log in using a social media account (such as Google or Facebook), we may receive certain profile information from that provider, including your name, email address, and profile picture, as described in Section 7.

Information Collected Automatically
When you use our Services, we may automatically collect:
Internet Protocol (IP) address and device identifiers
Browser type, operating system, and device type
Pages viewed, links clicked, and usage patterns
Referring URLs and session duration
Geolocation data (if you grant permission)

If you use our mobile app, we may request access to:
Geolocation — to provide location-relevant job matches (you can disable this in device settings)
Push notifications — for account updates and job alerts (you can disable this in device settings)

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information (such as racial or ethnic origin, health data, or religious beliefs). However, users may voluntarily include such information in their profiles. If you choose to do so, you consent to our processing of that information solely to deliver our Services.

All personal information you provide must be accurate and up to date. You are responsible for notifying us of any changes.

3. WHAT LEGAL BASES DO WE RELY ON?
In Short: We only process your personal information when we have a valid legal reason to do so under applicable law.


Depending on your location, we rely on the following legal bases:
Consent: Consent — where you have given us explicit permission for a specific purpose. You may withdraw consent at any time.
Contract: Performance of a Contract — where processing is necessary to provide the Services you have requested.
Legal Obligations: Legal Obligations — where processing is necessary to comply with applicable laws or regulatory requirements.
Legitimate Interests: Legitimate Interests — where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided these are not overridden by your rights.
Vital Interests: Vital Interests — in rare cases, where processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of a person.

If you are located in the EU or UK, the GDPR and UK GDPR apply. If you are in Canada, Canada's PIPEDA applies. Applicable legal bases are determined per the relevant jurisdiction.

In Short: Yes. AI is central to ZedReq's value proposition. We are transparent about how it works and what it means for your data.


ZedReq's core platform features are powered by artificial intelligence, including:
AI-driven job matching based on your profile and preferences
Skill gap analysis to identify areas for professional development
Personalized learning and course recommendations
Realistic AI-powered mock interviews and feedback
Natural language processing to understand your career goals

Your inputs (profile data, responses, activity) are processed by AI systems to generate personalized outputs and recommendations. These may involve inferences about your career trajectory, skills, and professional fit.

We do not use your personal data to train general AI models. We may use aggregated, anonymized, and de-identified data to improve our platform's AI systems, in a manner that cannot be traced back to you.
