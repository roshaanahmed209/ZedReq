  • lms_course_sections → lms_questions
  • lms_questions → lms_user_answers

ONE-TO-ONE (1:1):
  • users → lms_dashboard

  • users ↔ lms_domains (via lms_user_domain_performance)
  • users ↔ lms_course_sections (via lms_user_section_progress)
